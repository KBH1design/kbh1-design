KBH1 Design — James Cross, art director and brand designer

I’m James Cross, an independent art director and brand designer in Copenhagen. For 20 years I’ve helped organisations explain complex things simply and look like themselves while doing it.

See services Book a 20-minute call

Structure and intuition,
in the same room

I’ve worked in agencies, inside global technology brands and on my own independent projects. That mix taught me to think like a strategist and work like a maker. I care about the big idea and the last kerning pair, and I use new tools, including AI, without losing the human judgement that makes work worth looking at.

Four ways to work together

  • From 12,000 DKK

    Brand Audit

    See your brand clearly. A fixed-price, outside look at how your brand appears across web, print, social and space. You get an honest read on what works, what’s drifting and what to fix first.

  • From 45,000 DKK

    Brand Identity Sprint

    A new identity, without the endless process. A focused few weeks to build or renew your visual identity: logo, type, colour, image style and the core assets your team needs on day one.

  • From 15,000 / month DKK

    Art Direction Partner

    A senior creative eye, on call. Ongoing creative direction for teams producing a lot of content. I keep the work consistent and sharp, so your brand looks the same on its busiest day as on its best.

    Identity for Education

    Schools people are proud to walk into. Visual identity, signage and wayfinding, website refreshes and shared asset libraries for schools and colleges, built so staff can actually use them.

How I work

  1. Look

    We start by listening and auditing what exists. No assumptions.

  3. Build
